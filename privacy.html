隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-02

1. 我们访问哪些数据 / What we access

用户在聚宝邮中用 Google 账号授权后，软件可以访问该 Gmail 邮箱的邮件内容、邮件元数据（发件人、收件人、主题、时间）和邮箱地址。

When a user authorizes JubaoMail with their Google account, the app can access that Gmail mailbox's messages, message metadata (sender, recipients, subject, date) and the account's email address.

2. 用途 / How we use it

这些数据只用于软件本身的功能：在统一收件箱显示来信、提醒新邮件、由用户发送和回复邮件、整理邮件（如移到已删除）。不用于广告，不出售，不与第三方共享，也不用于训练人工智能模型。

The data is used only to provide the app's features: showing incoming mail in a unified inbox, notifying of new mail, sending and replying to email at the user's request, and organizing mail (such as moving to trash). It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used to train AI models.

JubaoMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. 存储与安全 / Storage and security

授权令牌和邮件数据保存在用户自己的电脑和本公司自己管理的服务器上，令牌加密保存。退出登录时会清除本机的登录信息。

Authorization tokens and mail data are stored on the user's own computer and on servers operated by our company; tokens are stored encrypted. Signing out clears the local sign-in data.

4. 撤销与删除 / Revoking access and deletion

用户可以随时在 Google 账号权限页面 撤销聚宝邮的访问权限，也可以发邮件要求删除我们保存的相关数据。

Users can revoke JubaoMail's access at any time at Google Account permissions, and may email us to request deletion of stored data.
